Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Jan 10, 2022
Last Updated: June 21, 2026

At Dizajnox - Design Dreams , we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of the data shared with us by our clients, investors, contractors, subcontractors, and website visitors.This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit requests for construction bids, purchase materials, rent heavy machinery, apply for workforce mobilization, or invest in our real estate portfolios (such as our Valley View project).

1. Information We Collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as data that is collected automatically through your interaction with our digital platforms.

A. Personal & Business Information You Provide 
1.1  Contact Details: Name, email address, physical mailing address, and phone number.
1.2 Business & Corporate Data: Company name, corporate tax identifiers, commercial licenses, and project specifications (e.g., architectural blueprints, site coordinates).
1.3 Financial & Investment Data: Bank account details for processing material or equipment rental invoices, billing records, and accredited investor documentation required for our joint-venture real estate opportunities.
1.4 Labor & Employment Data: Certification records, medical clearances, and identity verification documents submitted for workforce mobilization registration.

B. Information Collected Automatically
1.5 Log and Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, operating systems, and pages viewed on our website via standard tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information
We utilize your data under strict operational guidelines to execute our services and fulfill our contracts. Specifically, we use your information to:

2.1  Execute Core Services: Generate accurate architectural or construction bids, manage raw material supply chains, dispatch rental machinery, and coordinate on-site labor deployments.
2.2 Manage Investments: Process equity allocations, distribute property portfolio pro-formas, and manage rental pool payouts for real estate assets like our luxury cottage developments.
2.3 Streamline Communications: Send time-sensitive job-site updates, mechanical inspection records for rented equipment, invoicing, and requested project brochures.
2.4 Maintain Compliance: Enforce safety compliance, comply with municipal permitting laws, verify mining/site safety mandates, and fulfill regional tax and financial auditing requirements.

3. How We Share and Disclose Your Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. We only share information under the following strict conditions:

3.1 With Subcontractors & Site Partners:
We share necessary project data with vetted sub-contractors, transport fleets, and structural engineers exclusively to execute construction work on your active job sites.
3.2 With Regulatory & Legal Authorities:
We disclose data when legally required to secure local building permits, clear municipal zoning approvals, or comply with mandatory OSHA/safety investigations.
3.3 With Financial & Legal Trustees: For our real estate investment partners, data is securely shared with escrow agents, legal counsel, and banking institutions to process clean property deeds and asset registries.
3.4 Business Transfers: If our company undergoes a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the successor entity under the same privacy protections.

4. Data Security & Storage Architecture
We treat digital security with the same engineering discipline we bring to our physical construction foundations.

4.1 Protection Systems:
We utilize industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS) encryption across our website forms, alongside encrypted database repositories to protect your financial, blueprint, and corporate files.
4.2 Access Control:
Personnel access to sensitive files (such as investment data or labor background checks) is strictly limited to authorized project managers and compliance officers on a need-to-know basis.
4.3 Disclaimer: While we deploy rigorous safeguards, no digital storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
Our website uses cookies and similar tracking tools to optimize your browsing experience, analyze traffic flow, and track user engagement across our service pages. You can modify your internet browser settings to decline cookies; however, doing so may cause certain interactive features of our quotation forms or portal dashboards to function improperly.

6. Your Rights and Choices
Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 The Right to Access: You may request a comprehensive copy of the personal or corporate data we hold on file for your account.
6.2 The Right to Correction: You may request updates or corrections to inaccurate or incomplete project, financial, or contact data.
6.3 The Right to Deletion: You may request that we erase your personal data, provided that the data is no longer necessary to fulfill active construction contracts, resolve open financial balances, or satisfy mandatory regulatory/tax retention laws.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect operational updates, new service integrations, or shifts in regional privacy regulations. Any updates will be designated by an updated "Last Modified" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
