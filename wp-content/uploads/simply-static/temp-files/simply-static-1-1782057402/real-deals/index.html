Real Estate Deals & Policy Agreement

Clear Policies for Confident Property Ownership.

Real Estate Policy 

Effective Date: Jan 10, 2022
Last Updated: June 21, 2026

Welcome to the Real Estate & Property Development division of Dizajnox - Design Dreams ™. This Real Estate Deals & Policy Agreement ("Agreement") outlines the standard operating policies, transaction frameworks, reservation mechanics, and cancellation rules governing all real estate asset acquisitions, plot sales, and cottage development deals managed by our firm—including our master-planned community projects like Valley View (Bamoli, Lansdowne, Uttarakhand).By submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI), placing a booking deposit, or participating in our property allocation cycles, you express your full understanding of and agreement to the policies detailed below.

1. Expression of Interest (EOI) & Reservation Policy
To maintain the exclusivity of our developments and secure prime inventory (such as premium valley-facing plots or custom cottage footprints), properties are reserved using a phased intake system.

1.1 The Booking Deposit: A property is only taken off the open market once a formal Expression of Interest (EOI) form is submitted alongside a specified, cleared reservation token or booking deposit.
1.2 Holding Period: Upon receiving the EOI and booking token, the Company will freeze the selected plot or cottage unit for a standard period of fifteen (15) calendar days. This window is granted to allow the buyer to review land titles, finalize financial liquidity, and execute the formal Allotment Agreement.
1.3 Inventory Forfeiture: If the formal Allotment Agreement is not signed, or the mandatory first-stage milestone payment is not cleared within the specified holding window, the reservation expires automatically, and the property unit will be released back into the active public inventory pool.

2. Pricing, Inclusions, and Statutory Taxes
We stand for absolute financial transparency. Every real estate deal is meticulously broken down to avoid hidden overheads:

2.1 Base Sale Price (BSP): The advertised or agreed-upon price covers the cost of the freehold land plot or the physical core construction of the cottage as detailed in your specific structural contract.
2.2 Infrastructure & External Development Charges (EDC): Unless explicitly stated as bundled, separate line-item charges apply for community infrastructure development. This includes building internal paved roads, installing perimeter gated security, engineering heavy retaining walls (essential for mountain terrains like Lansdowne), and running main utility conduits (water and electricity grids).
2.3 Statutory Taxes & Government Fees: The buyer is solely responsible for clearing all applicable regional taxes, Goods and Services Tax (GST), stamp duties, registry processing fees, and local municipal corporation levies at the time of legal title conveyance.

4. Cancellation, Refund, and Exit Policies
We understand that personal financial plans can shift. We offer a fair, standardized cancellation policy to protect both our investors and our operational timelines:

4.1 Cancellation Within the Holding Window: If a buyer cancels their reservation in writing within fifteen (15) days of submitting their initial EOI—and prior to executing the formal Allotment Agreement—the booking token is fully refundable, minus a minor 10% administrative and processing fee.
4.2 Cancellation Post-Agreement: Once a formal Allotment Agreement is signed and construction materials or site infrastructure assets are mobilized for that unit, cancellations are governed strictly by the clauses embedded within that contract. Typically, an earnest-money component (usually 10% of the total property value) is deemed non-refundable to offset land-holding costs and asset-demobilization overheads.

5. Delays, Force Majeure, and Mitigation Protocols
As a vertically integrated firm, we mitigate traditional real estate delays by operating our own heavy excavation machinery, maintaining certified on-demand labor forces, and managing our own construction material supply lines.

5.1 Commitment to Timelines: We back our build schedules with clear completion targets.
5.2 Force Majeure Exceptions: The Company shall not be held liable for construction or key-handover delays resulting directly from events beyond our rational control. These include severe hill-station weather anomalies (such as extended cloudbursts or regional monsoonal road closures in Uttarakhand), delayed municipal or state environmental zoning clearances, or national material logistics strikes. In such cases, construction timelines are extended linearly with the duration of the disruptive event.

6. The Managed Rental Pool Policy
For buyers participating in our optional Luxury Vacation Rental Pool program (designed to generate passive short-term homestay income when you are not using your cottage):

6.1 Hospitality Standards: To maintain the premium branding required to fetch top-tier nightly rental rates, all participating cottages must conform to a unified interior design specification, premium furnishing profile, and professional maintenance standard approved by our management team.
6.2 Revenue Distribution: Total net rental revenues (after booking platform commissions, local utility costs, housekeeping wages, and estate maintenance fees are deducted) are distributed directly to cottage owners on a structured quarterly basis. Detailed ledger statements are made available via your secure online investor dashboard.

7. Legal Title Conveyance and Governance

7.1 Title Integrity: The Company guarantees that all freehold plots and cottage developments offered are backed by clear, unencumbered legal titles, vetted thoroughly by independent legal counsel.
7.2 Jurisdiction: This Agreement and all subsequent real estate transactions shall be governed by and interpreted under the laws of the state where the property asset is geographically situated (e.g., for Valley View, the courts of Uttarakhand hold sole primary jurisdiction).
